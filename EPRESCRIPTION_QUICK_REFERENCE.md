# E-Prescription Quick Reference Guide for Doctors

## The 4-Step Process

### ✅ STEP 1: End Your Consultation
```
During Video Conference
└─ Click the RED "End Consultation" button (top-right corner)
   └─ A dialog appears asking how you want to end
```

**Choose:** ✅ **"Completed"** (to fill e-prescription)
- This records that you finished the consultation normally

---

### 📝 STEP 2: Fill E-Prescription Form
```
E-Prescription Form Opens (you'll see "STEP 2 of 2")
└─ Auto-filled fields:
   ├─ Your name & specialty
   ├─ Patient name
   └─ Consultation date
```

**Add Medications:**
1. **Medication Name** (required) → Example: "Paracetamol"
2. **Dosage** (required) → Example: "500mg" or "1 tablet"
3. **Frequency** (required) → Pick from dropdown
   - Once daily / Twice daily / Three times daily / etc.
4. **Duration** (optional) → Example: "5 days" or "2 weeks"
5. **Instructions** (optional) → Example: "Take with food"

Click **"Add Medication"** → Medication appears in list below

**Repeat for each medication the patient needs**

---

### ✔️ STEP 3: Review & Submit
```
After adding all medications:
└─ Click "Review & Submit (X)" button
   where X = number of medications
```

**Confirmation Dialog appears:**
- Shows all your medications in the form above
- Confirms patient will see this immediately
- Two options:
  - **"Back to Edit"** → Make changes
  - **"Confirm & Submit"** → Final submission

Click **"Confirm & Submit"** → Submission processes

---

### ✨ STEP 4: See Success & Return to Dashboard
```
Success Screen appears for 2 seconds:
├─ Green checkmark icon ✓
├─ "Success!" message
├─ Patient name confirmation
└─ Auto-redirects to your Dashboard
```

**That's it!** Patient can now see this prescription in their Consultation Log.

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I add multiple medications?
**A:** Yes! Add as many as needed. Each medication must have name, dosage, and frequency filled in.

### Q: What if I make a mistake?
**A:** Before submitting, click "Back to Edit" in the confirmation dialog. After submission, contact admin.

### Q: Can I cancel after starting the e-prescription form?
**A:** Yes, click "Cancel" button at the bottom. You'll need to fill it again later.

### Q: What happens if my internet cuts out during submission?
**A:** The form will show an error. Try submitting again - it usually works on the second attempt.

### Q: When can the patient see the prescription?
**A:** Immediately after you submit it! They can see it in their Consultation Log.

### Q: Can I print the prescription?
**A:** Patient can print it from their Consultation Log.

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **DO:**
- Fill in all required fields before clicking "Add Medication"
- Review all medications before final submission
- Wait for success screen before closing or navigating
- Add special instructions for complex medications

❌ **DON'T:**
- Close the form during submission (it will lose data)
- Submit without at least one medication
- Skip required fields (form won't let you)
- Refresh the page during the process

## Error Messages & Solutions

| Error | What to Do |
|-------|-----------|
| "Medication name is required" | Fill in the medication name |
| "Dosage is required" | Fill in the dosage (e.g., 500mg) |
| "Frequency is required" | Select a frequency from dropdown |
| "Please add at least one medication" | Add at least one complete medication |
| "Failed to submit e-prescription" | Check internet, then try "Confirm & Submit" again |

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **TAB** → Move to next field
- **ENTER** in form → Cannot submit (click buttons instead)
- **ESC** → Close form (same as Cancel button)
